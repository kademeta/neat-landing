Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

NEAT is a 100% local Chrome extension. All processing happens directly in your browser.

What We Collect

We collect nothing from you. NEAT does not send any data to our servers or any third parties.

What NEAT Does With Your Data

  • NEAT reads your prompt locally when you type or paste in Grok, ChatGPT, or Claude.
  • It compresses the prompt and redacts personally identifiable information (PII) entirely in your browser.
  • The processed prompt is then sent to the AI service you are using.

Local Storage

NEAT uses Chrome’s local storage only to save:

  • Your compression preferences (Smart or Vibe Mode)
  • Auto-send setting
  • Compression history and Insights data
  • Pro access status (if you have lifetime access)

All of this data stays on your device only.

No Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.

Your Rights

You can clear all stored data at any time from the Insights tab in the extension.

If you have any questions, contact us at: support@getneat.io
