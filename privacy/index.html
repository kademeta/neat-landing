Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

NEAT is a 100% local Chrome extension. All processing (compression and PII redaction) happens directly in your browser on your device. No data is ever sent to our servers.

What Data NEAT Collects

By default, NEAT collects **no personal data**. It only processes your prompts locally and then sends the optimized version to the AI service you are using (Grok, ChatGPT, or Claude).

Local Storage

NEAT stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome storage:

  • Your compression preferences (Smart or Vibe Mode)
  • Auto-send setting
  • Compression history and Insights statistics
  • Pro / lifetime access status

Beta Testing & Feedback

During the private beta period, users may optionally share compressed prompts, screenshots, or feedback with the developer by copying data to their clipboard. This is completely voluntary and used only to improve the extension.

Users may also optionally provide a Dogecoin wallet address to receive a thank-you Doge inscription from the developer.

No Data Selling or Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties.

Your Rights

You can clear all locally stored data at any time from the Insights tab in the extension.
